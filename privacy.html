Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Short version: the app has no login and collects no name or email. The conversation text or screenshots you choose to get help with are sent to our AI provider to generate suggestions, and then not stored on our servers. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

This policy explains how the texting. app ("texting.", "we", "us") handles information. texting. is an individual-developer app published by Aarav Kataria. By using the app you agree to this policy.

What we handle

Content you submit for suggestions

When you paste or type a conversation, or add a screenshot, that content is sent to our AI provider so the app can generate reply suggestions. If you add a screenshot, the image is processed to read the conversation it shows. We treat this content as sensitive and use it only to produce your suggestions.

Optional "sound like you" sample

If you choose to connect a chat export so replies match your phrasing, that text is used the same way — to generate suggestions in your voice. This step is optional and can be skipped; the app works without it.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of information on your device only — your appearance preference, whether you've finished onboarding, your remaining free uses, and your subscription status. This never leaves your phone except as needed to verify your subscription (below), and it is removed when you delete the app.

What we do not collect

  • No account, username, or password — the app has no login.
  • No name, email address, or phone number collected by the app.
  • No contacts, location, or advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

How your content is processed

Suggestions are generated by a large language model operated by our AI provider, Anthropic, reached through our own secure server, which passes your content to the provider and returns the suggestions. Our server does not store the conversations you submit. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, inputs sent through the API are not used to train their models and are retained only transiently for operational and safety purposes. See Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store; we never see or receive your payment-card details. We use RevenueCat to verify and manage subscription status. For this, a random app-generated identifier and your purchase receipt are processed so the app knows whether your subscription is active. See revenuecat.com/privacy and apple.com/legal/privacy.

Who we share with

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. Content and identifiers are shared only with the service providers needed to run the app:

  • Anthropic — to generate reply suggestions from the content you submit.
  • RevenueCat and Apple — to process and verify your subscription.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of our users and the service.

Retention

We don't keep your submitted conversations on our servers after your suggestions are returned. Information stored on your device stays until you clear it or delete the app. Our providers retain data per their own policies linked above.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data. Because the app stores data on your device, deleting the app (or using "Delete my data" in Settings) removes it. To request deletion of anything held by a provider, contact us and we'll help.
  • Skip the voice sample. You can use the app without connecting any chats.
  • Manage your subscription. In the iOS Settings app under your Apple ID, or in-app via Settings → Manage subscription.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Contact us to exercise them.

Children

texting. is rated 17+ and is not intended for anyone under 17. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Content is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change and revise the date above. Significant changes will be reflected in the app.
