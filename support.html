Frequently asked

How does texting. come up with replies?

You share a conversation by typing, pasting, or adding a screenshot, then pick where you want it to go. The app sends that to an AI model that drafts three replies built on frameworks for natural, genuine conversation. You choose what to send, if anything.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no login. The app works right away and keeps your settings on your device.

What's free, and what's Pro?

You get a few free suggestions to try it. Pro unlocks unlimited suggestions, screenshot reading, and voice matching as it improves. Prices are shown in the app before you subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside billing on our end. Open the iOS Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → texting. → Cancel Subscription. You can also reach this in-app via Settings → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

If you reinstalled or switched devices, open Settings → Restore purchases in the app while signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

I want a refund.

Purchases go through the App Store, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue App Store refunds directly, but email us if you need a hand.

How do I delete my data?

The app stores data on your device, so deleting the app removes it. You can also use Settings → Delete my data in the app. We don't keep the conversations you submit on our servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Are my conversations private?

The content you submit is used only to generate your suggestions and isn't stored on our servers or used for advertising. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
